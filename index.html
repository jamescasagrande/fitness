Monday
Lower Body A — Hinge, Single-Leg, Core
Disc note: No axial spinal loading. Hip-hinge and single-leg patterns only. If you feel any lumbar discomfort or nerve referral during RDLs, stop that exercise — don't push through it.

Compounds — protect these

ExerciseSets × RepsRest
Romanian Deadlift (dumbbells)
Hip hinge; soft knee; lumbar neutral throughout. Stop descent when you feel hamstring tension, not when the weight hits the floor.
Compound 		4 × 8–10 90 s
Bulgarian Split Squat (dumbbells)
Rear foot elevated on bench. Lower slowly — 3 sec down. Drop weight before form breaks; lumbar rounding here is a disc risk.
Compound 		3 × 10 / leg 90 s

Accessories — cut here if short on time

ExerciseSets × RepsRest
Leg Curl (machine)
Controlled eccentric; don't let hips rise off pad
Accessory 		3 × 12 60 s
Cable Hip Abduction
Ankle attachment, low cable. Stand sideways; sweep leg out. Slow and controlled — this is not a power movement. Addresses a runner-specific gap.
Added — Runner Priority 		3 × 15 / side 45 s
Standing Calf Raise (dumbbell)
Accessory 		3 × 15–20 45 s

Core — do not skip; most important block for disc health

ExerciseSets × RepsRest
Dead Bug
Lie on back, arms up, knees at 90°. Lower opposite arm and leg slowly while keeping lumbar pressed flat to floor. If your back arches, reduce range of motion. Best anterior core exercise for disc history — trains spine to resist extension under load.
Added — Disc Priority 		3 × 8 / side 45 s
Pallof Press (cable)
Set cable at chest height. Stand sideways, press straight out and hold briefly. Resist rotation — that's the entire point. Trains anti-rotation which running demands constantly.
Added — Disc + Runner Priority 		3 × 10 / side 45 s

Neutral grip; knee and hand on bench; pull toward hip not chest; don't rotate trunk excessively

Wednesday
Run — Easy Aerobic / Zone 2
Duration30–45 min EffortZone 2 HR60–70% max

Conversational pace throughout. You should be able to speak in full sentences. This is mid-week aerobic maintenance — not a hard effort. Drifting into zone 3 reduces the aerobic adaptation value and adds fatigue you'll carry into Thursday's lifting.

Post-run (5–10 min)

Walking cool-down. Hip flexor stretch, calf stretch, figure-4 or pigeon for glutes. No lifting today.

Upper Body B — Pull / Push / Core

Compounds — protect these

ExerciseSets × RepsRest
Wide-Grip Pulldown
Pronated grip, hands just outside shoulder width. Full stretch at top; drive elbows down and back. Kept here as the primary vertical pull.

Bench at 30–40°; brief pause at chest; different angle from Tuesday machine press
Compound 		4 × 10 90 s

Pronated grip; add KB between feet if needed. If fatigue is high from pulldowns, treat this as a quality set — reduce load rather than grinding ugly reps.

Core — 8 min; do not skip

ExerciseSets × RepsRest
Plank
Forearms or hands. Neutral spine — don't let hips sag or pike. If 45 s is easy, add a reach or shoulder tap rather than just holding longer.

Rotational core; stand sideways to cable, sweep diagonally. Complements Pallof press (anti-rotation on Mon) with controlled rotation here.

Run took < 35 min → Do full Lower B as written
Run took 35–45 min → Do hip thrust + back extension only; skip the rest
Legs felt dead on the run → Skip lifting entirely; it's a running day
High mileage week → Skip lifting entirely
TypeLong Run Duration35–45 min EffortEasy / Z2

Your weekly long(ish) run. Easy effort — time on feet, not speed. This is the priority of the day; lifting is secondary.

Lower Body B — Glute & Hip (post-run)

ExerciseSets × RepsRest
Hip Thrust (dumbbell or KB)
Shoulders on bench; load across hips; full glute squeeze at top. Low spinal load — appropriate even post-run.
Compound 		3 × 12–15 60 s
Roman Chair Back Extension (KB hold)
Hold KB at chest. Go lighter than usual — spinal erectors are already working from the run. Stop if lumbar feels fatigued.
Compound 		3 × 12 60 s
Single-Leg RDL (KB)
Balance + posterior chain + hip stability. Only do this if time and energy permit after the above two.
Accessory — time permitting 		2 × 10 / leg 60 s

Disc Herniation

Leg press, barbell squats, and axial loading excluded. Hip thrusts, RDLs, split squats are lower-risk but not zero-risk. Back off immediately at any nerve referral or lumbar pain. These are general principles — not a substitute for PT clearance.

Core Is Not Optional

Dead bug and Pallof press on Monday, plank and woodchop on Thursday. These are the most clinically relevant additions for your disc history and for running. Cut other accessories first.

Hip Abductors

Cable hip abduction on Monday targets glute med — a common weakness in runners that contributes to IT band issues and knee valgus. Easy to skip, worth keeping.

Pull Balance

Tue now uses cable row (horizontal pull) instead of pulldown. Thu has pulldown + pull-ups (both vertical). Week total: 2 vertical pulls, 2 horizontal pulls, plus single-arm rows. More balanced than v2.

50 lb Ceiling

When you outgrow a dumbbell movement, slow the eccentric to 3–4 seconds before adding accessories. Cables can often fill the gap.

Weekend

Sat/Sun off. Optional easy walk, mobility, or foam rolling. A short easy run on one weekend day is fine if you want more mileage — keep it zone 2.
